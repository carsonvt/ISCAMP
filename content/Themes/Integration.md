Military integration refers to the process of aligning the capabilities, structures, and operations of multiple military forces—whether from different nations, service branches, or allied organizations—to function as a cohesive and interoperable force. This can range from joint training exercises and shared logistics to fully integrated command structures and multinational combat operations. Successful military integration enhances operational effectiveness, strengthens deterrence, and improves crisis response capabilities.

Military integration hinges on several key aspects: 
- [[Interoperability]]
- [[Joint Training & Military Exercises]]
- [[Unified Command Structures]]
- [[Logistics & Resource Sharing]]
- [[Strategic & Tactical Coordination]]

While military integration strengthens alliances and coalition effectiveness, it also presents challenges such as political sensitivities, sovereignty concerns, differences in military culture, and technical compatibility issues. Balancing integration with national autonomy remains a key consideration in defense cooperation.
