## Overview 

The Brazil–Argentina military partnership is characterized by regional defense integration, [[Arms Agreements|joint arms development]], and diplomatic balancing between historical rivalries and modern security cooperation. As the two largest military powers in [[South America]], Brazil and Argentina engage in [[Peacekeeping Operations|peacekeeping operations]], aerospace collaboration, and defense industry integration, despite political and economic tensions.
## History

- Rivalry & Military Competition (1800s–1980s): Brazil and Argentina historically competed for regional influence, leading to defense spending races and border disputes.
- [[Mercosur]] & Regional Security Integration (1990s–2010s): Economic integration under Mercosur facilitated military cooperation and confidence-building measures.
- Joint Defense Industry & Space Collaboration (2010s–Present): Both nations co-develop military aircraft, naval assets, and aerospace technologies, strengthening regional security.
## Characteristics & Initiatives

- Defense Industry & Military Technology Development: Brazil and Argentina co-develop KC-390 military transport aircraft and naval systems, boosting regional defense capabilities. Argentina has expressed interest in acquiring Brazilian-built armored vehicles and advanced fighter jets.
- Joint Military Training & Peacekeeping Operations: Both nations contribute troops to [[UN Peacekeeping Missions|UN peacekeeping missions]] and conduct [[Joint Training & Military Exercises|joint army and naval exercises]]. Argentina participates in Brazil-led Amazonian security initiatives, reinforcing border defense cooperation.
- Strategic Maritime & Aerospace Collaboration: Brazil and Argentina coordinate in South Atlantic security, countering illegal fishing and transnational crime. The partnership extends to joint space programs, focusing on satellite surveillance and aerospace defense.
## Strategic Importance

- Strengthening South American Defense Autonomy: The partnership reduces reliance on foreign military imports by enhancing regional arms production.
- Naval & Air Force Interoperability: Joint training and defense collaboration improve regional force readiness.
- Mercosur Defense & Security Framework: Both nations push for greater military integration within [[Latin America]], ensuring regional stability.
