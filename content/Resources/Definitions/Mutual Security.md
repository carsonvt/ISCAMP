Mutual security refers to a cooperative defense arrangement in which two or more countries commit to protecting each other against common threats. 

This concept underpins most [[Bilateral Alliances|bilateral]] and [[Multilateral Military Alliances|multilateral]] military alliances, best embodied by [[North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO)|NATO's Article 5]], which stipulates that an attack on one is considered an attack on all. Mutual security arrangements can involve [[Joint Training & Military Exercises|joint military exercises]], [[Intelligence|intelligence sharing]], [[Arms Agreements|arms agreements]], and coordinated responses to crises. 

By presenting a unified front, military partners enhance deterrence and reduce the likelihood of aggression from adversaries.
