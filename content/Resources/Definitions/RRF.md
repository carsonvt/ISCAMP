A **rapid response force** is a military unit specifically organized, trained, and equipped to **deploy quickly** in response to emerging crises—ranging from armed conflict and natural disasters to humanitarian emergencies and counterterrorism operations. In the current international security environment, these forces are often **multinational**, **modular**, and **high-readiness**, designed to project stability, deter aggression, or provide immediate relief with minimal notice.

**Key Attributes**
- High readiness and mobility
- Scalable and mission-adaptable (combat, peacekeeping, disaster response)
- Often jointly or multinationally composed (e.g., [[NATO Response Force]], [[EU Rapid Deployment Capacity]], [[ASF|African Union Standby Force]])
