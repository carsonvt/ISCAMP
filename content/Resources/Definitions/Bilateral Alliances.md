A bilateral alliance is a formal defense agreement between two countries. Often built upon foundational commitments to [[Mutual Security|mutual security]], cooperation, and military coordination, alliances may also involve [[Defense Pacts|defense pacts]], [[Joint Training & Military Exercises|joint training exercises]], [[Intelligence|intelligence sharing]], [[Arms Agreements|arms agreements]], and [[Logistics & Resource Sharing|logistical support]]. 

Unlike [[Multilateral Military Alliances|multilateral alliances]], which involve multiple nations, bilateral alliances focus on deepening strategic ties between two partners, often reflecting shared [[Military Interests|security interests]], regional stability concerns, or [[Historical Ties in Coalition Warfare|historical ties]].
