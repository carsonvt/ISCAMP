## Overview

Formed at [[Arcadia Conference|Arcadia]], CSAB was supposed to be the central Allied board for coordinating shipping capacity and movements between the U.S. and Britain. In practice, it was bypassed due to inter-service rivalries and the failure to agree on a pooling mechanism.
## Composition

- U.S.: Admiral Emory Land (War Shipping Administration)
- UK: Sir Arthur Salter (Ministry of War Transport)
## Failures & Controversies

- No control over military movements; Army and Navy retained separate authority
- Internal U.S. turf wars blocked effectiveness
- Direct negotiations between British officials and FDR sidelined CSAB
- Formally replaced in practice by the United Maritime Authority in 1944
## Analysis

- Illustrates the fragility of interagency cooperation in wartime U.S.    
- One of the clearest failures of the [[Combination|combined system]] due to overlapping institutional mandates​
