## Overview 

The CFB was a commodity board formed to prevent competitive purchasing of food on global markets, especially from neutral nations. It played a critical role in wartime resource stabilization and laid groundwork for postwar humanitarian relief.
## Operations

- Worked through London-based subcommittees (e.g., Tea Allocation Committee)
- Collaborated with Empire countries through the London Food Council
- Coordinated with other Boards during crises like Operation Torch (1942–43)
## Contributions

- Oversaw purchases and allocations from key suppliers (e.g., Latin America)
- Functioned through close personal ties between ministries
- Influenced early design of UNRRA logistics models
## Legacy

- Prototype for international food security cooperation
- Demonstrated the importance of economic coordination in coalition warfare​
